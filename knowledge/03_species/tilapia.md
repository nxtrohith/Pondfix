# Nile tilapia (Oreochromis niloticus)

A warm-water species that tolerates a range of conditions. Tolerance does not mean poor water quality is harmless: stocking, feeding, temperature and oxygen still need management. Use only locally permitted species and suitable culture practice.

## Provisional working ranges (reference only, not validated alert limits)
| Parameter | Working range | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temperature | about 25-30 C | FAO guidance for preferred growth |
| pH | about 6.5-8.5 | FAO guidance |
| Dissolved oxygen | about 5-6 mg/L or more | proposed early warning below 4, high priority below 3 mg/L |
| Un-ionized NH3 | keep very low | see the ammonia guide |

PondFix has no reference ranges configured for tilapia yet.

## Diseases to know
`streptococcosis`, `tilapia_lake_virus`, `columnaris`, `saprolegniosis`. None can be diagnosed from activity, temperature or pH alone.

## Using combined signals
- Several fish with lasting low activity plus rising temperature: possible environmental stress event.
- One fish with persistent erratic swimming while the pond readings are stable: individual fish-health concern.
- Neither proves a disease. Offer plausible explanations and ways to check.
