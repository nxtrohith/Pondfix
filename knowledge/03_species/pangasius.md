# Pangasius (Pangasianodon hypophthalmus)

A commercially important freshwater species. Note: the PondFix catalogue has not yet fixed the exact Pangasius species. Configure ranges for your actual strain, stocking density and system rather than copying carp or tilapia values.

## Initial engineering defaults (not experimentally validated, not alert limits)
| Parameter | Initial target | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temperature | about 26-30 C | validate locally |
| pH | about 6.5-8.5 | general freshwater starting range |
| Dissolved oxygen | aim for 5 mg/L or more where practical | tolerating low oxygen is not the same as good welfare |
| Un-ionized NH3 | keep very low | depends on pH and temperature |
| Nitrite | keep low | needs its own test |
| Water level | pond-specific | depends on density and geometry |

## Health risks
- **Environmental stress**: poor feeding, odd swimming, low activity, breathing distress, deaths. Keep aeration, avoid overstocking, watch oxygen and nitrogen compounds, remove uneaten feed.
- **Bacterial infections**: skin lesions, ulcers, systemic illness; presentation varies. Keep biosecurity and get a professional diagnosis.
- **Parasites**: inspect new stock, quarantine, and confirm before treating.
