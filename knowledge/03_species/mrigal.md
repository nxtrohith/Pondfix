# Mrigal (Cirrhinus mrigala)

A bottom-oriented Indian major carp, common in composite carp culture. Pond-bottom condition, organic build-up and feed management matter especially for it.

## Provisional working ranges (reference only, not validated alert limits)
| Parameter | Working range | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temperature | about 24-31 C in this library | the PondFix species catalogue currently shows 22-32 C; the two differ and need expert reconciliation. The pond's configured setting takes precedence. |
| pH | about 6.5-8.5 | general starting range |
| Dissolved oxygen | aim for 5 mg/L or more | proposed early warning below 4, high priority below 3 mg/L |
| Un-ionized NH3 | keep very low | see the ammonia guide |

## Health problems to know
- **Oxygen deficiency**: reduced activity, odd swimming, breathing distress. Aerate, check before dawn, avoid overstocking, remove organic waste.
- **Parasites** (Argulus, Lernaea, gill and skin parasites): susceptibility varies by parasite and population; do not assume every carp is affected equally.
- **Bacterial disease**: skin damage, ulcers, haemorrhages. Investigate unusual deaths promptly.

## Monitoring limits
Mrigal feeds near the bottom, so surface readings may miss bottom-water oxygen problems. A surface camera cannot see fish that stay out of its view.
