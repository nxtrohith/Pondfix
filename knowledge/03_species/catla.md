# Catla (Catla catla)

An Indian major carp commonly farmed with rohu and mrigal. It feeds high in the water column and eats a lot of zooplankton, so in compatible carp polyculture it uses a different niche from rohu and mrigal.

## Provisional working ranges (reference only, not validated alert limits)
| Parameter | Working range | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temperature | about 26-29 C | FAO-style optimal range for catla |
| pH | about 6.5-8.5 | general starting range |
| Dissolved oxygen | aim for 5 mg/L or more | proposed early warning below 4, high priority below 3 mg/L |
| Un-ionized NH3 | keep very low | see the ammonia guide |

## Health problems to know
- **Oxygen deficiency**: monitor swimming, surface crowding, breathing, feeding and deaths. Aerate, avoid overstocking, check at high-risk times.
- **Argulosis**: catla is susceptible. See `argulosis`.
- **Lernaeasis (anchor worm)**: ICAR-CIFA work identifies catla as a susceptible host. See `lernaeasis`.
- **Bacterial ulcers and haemorrhages**: signs are non-specific; see `aeromoniasis`.

## Approach
Use a catla-specific temperature baseline and the common rules for pH, oxygen and ammonia. Low movement alone does not mean parasites or bacteria; look for more evidence.
