# Walking catfish (Clarias batrachus)

An air-breathing freshwater fish. It can take oxygen from the air, so its oxygen needs and surface behaviour differ from carps. It is also predatory towards small fish (see compatibility).

## Initial working targets (not validated, not alert limits)
| Parameter | Initial target | Note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Temperature | about 26-30 C | validate locally |
| pH | about 6.5-8.5 | general starting range |
| Dissolved oxygen | aim for 3-5 mg/L or more where practical | proposed early warning below 3, high priority below 2 mg/L |
| Un-ionized NH3 | keep very low | air breathing does not protect against ammonia |
| Nitrite | keep low | measure independently |
| Water level | pond-specific | low water concentrates waste |

Being able to breathe air does not make low dissolved oxygen harmless.

## Behaviour caution
Surfacing alone is not proof of low oxygen in this species: it may be normal air breathing. Judge it against the species baseline and the surrounding readings. A model that flags every surface visit as abnormal will perform poorly here.

## Health risks
- **Water-quality stress**: poor feeding, odd swimming, weakness, more surfacing than usual.
- **Bacterial disease**: skin damage, ulcers, haemorrhages. Keep water quality, avoid handling injuries, get a diagnosis.
- **Parasites and water moulds**: skin and gill lesions, abnormal swimming. Inspect, keep biosecurity, remove dead fish promptly, and do not treat without confirming the cause.
