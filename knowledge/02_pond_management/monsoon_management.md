# Seasonal and monsoon management

Rain changes pond chemistry, temperature, depth and circulation.

## Risks
Diluted alkalinity and pH shifts, fast temperature change, overflow and fish escape, inflow of silt, pesticides or other contaminants, damaged embankments and inlets, cloudy days that cut photosynthesis while respiration continues, and mixing of oxygen-poor bottom water.

## Before heavy rain
1. Inspect embankments, spillways, screens and drains.
2. Secure inlets and outlets so stock cannot escape.
3. Test aerators, electrical safety and backup power.
4. Take baseline water-quality readings.
5. Remove built-up waste where appropriate.
6. Note stocking, recent feeding and known pond problems.

## During and after
- Check water level and embankments.
- Watch fish behaviour and feeding.
- Measure DO, pH and temperature when practical, and check the water source.
- Avoid sudden large water exchanges unless assessed.
- Reassess feeding if activity or water quality changes.

Seasonal advice should be reviewed for your region and not copied unchanged from another climate.
