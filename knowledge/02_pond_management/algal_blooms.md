# Algal blooms

Algae are a normal and useful part of pond productivity. The problem is excessive or unstable growth, or harmful species.

## Why blooms develop
Nutrients from feed and waste, fertilizer, warm weather and light, and changes in circulation or grazing.

## Risks
Large day-night oxygen swings, high afternoon pH, sudden oxygen loss when a bloom collapses, toxins from some species, and reduced clarity.

## Warning signs
Abrupt colour change, dense surface scum, sudden drop in feeding, distress near dawn, unexplained deaths. Colour alone cannot show whether a bloom is harmful.

## Response
1. Measure DO, including before dawn if you can.
2. Record weather, feeding and fertilizer inputs.
3. Avoid sudden unplanned chemical treatments: a mass algal kill can deplete oxygen.
4. Review nutrient loading, and get expert help if a harmful bloom is suspected.
5. Monitor more often while things change quickly.
