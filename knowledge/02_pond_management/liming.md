# Liming

Lime is used for specific purposes such as correcting soil acidity or managing alkalinity. The product and amount must match a soil and water analysis.

## Cautions
- Adding lime to high-pH water can make things worse: higher pH raises the toxic un-ionized ammonia fraction.
- Agricultural limestone, hydrated lime and quicklime behave differently and carry different hazards. Do not treat them as interchangeable.
- Do not add lime because of a single unusual pH reading.

## Before liming
Measure pH at a consistent time of day, alkalinity, and ammonia. Get a professional recommendation for the product and rate, and retest afterwards. This library gives no application rates.
