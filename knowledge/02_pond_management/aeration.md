# Aeration and emergency aeration

Aeration adds oxygen and mixes water. It is the first response when oxygen depletion is suspected.

## Emergency aeration
Start or increase suitable aeration when low oxygen is plausible and the equipment can be run safely. Check power supply, the equipment itself and animal behaviour. Put the effort where animals are most affected, without stirring up bottom sediment more than needed.

## If the aerator or power fails
1. Restore power or switch to backup if you have it.
2. Stop feeding.
3. Watch the surface for gasping. If distress appears, treat it as an emergency and call an expert.
4. Measure DO once it is safe to do so, and keep measuring until it is stable.

## Limits
Aeration helps oxygen and treatment but does not remove ammonia by itself, and it is not a cure for disease. Plan backup aeration before the monsoon and the hot season, and test it.
