# Water exchange

Replacing part of the water can help with some dissolved contaminants, but it is not a universal remedy.

## Before replacing water
Check the source water for temperature, pH, DO, salinity, possible pathogens and contamination, and local discharge rules. Large exchanges with water at a different temperature can shock the animals.

## Avoid
- Sudden large exchanges unless the source water has been assessed.
- Exchange during a suspected disease outbreak without advice, because water and equipment can spread infection.

## Use it with
Measurement first (ammonia, nitrite, pH, DO), then a decision. Recheck the pond after exchange.
