# Feeding and feed management

Feed grows fish and is also the biggest source of pond pollution. Excess feed raises oxygen use and ammonia; too little slows growth and weakens condition.

## A plan should consider
Species and life stage, average weight and total biomass, feed type and pellet size, water temperature and oxygen, appetite and actual consumption, stocking density and growth, and any disease, stress or recent deaths.

## Daily feed calculation
Daily feed (kg) = biomass (kg) x feeding rate (%) / 100.
Example only: 500 kg biomass at 2% is 10 kg per day. The 2% is illustrative, not a recommended rate for any species or stage.

## Good practice
- Keep a steady schedule suited to species and system, and adjust to what is actually eaten.
- Do not feed heavily during oxygen stress. Cut or pause feed when fish are distressed or water quality drops, then restart gradually when appetite returns.
- Store feed properly and discard spoiled feed safely.
- Recalculate as fish grow.
- Record feed, deaths, water quality and growth.

A sudden drop in feeding is a useful warning sign, but it does not say what the cause is.
