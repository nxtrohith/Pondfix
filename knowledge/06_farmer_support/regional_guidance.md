# Where to get regional help and trusted guidance

For farms in India, the most reliable guidance comes from official fisheries bodies and research institutes, then international technical references, then peer-reviewed research.

## Official and research sources
- ICAR-CIFA (Central Institute of Freshwater Aquaculture): publications and training for freshwater aquaculture. https://cifa.nic.in/od/epublication/
- Other ICAR fisheries institutes, and ICAR e-publications. https://epubs.icar.org.in/
- Your state fisheries department and local fisheries officer.

## International references
- FAO Fisheries and Aquaculture. https://www.fao.org/fishery/en
- WOAH Aquatic Animal Health Code. https://www.woah.org/en/what-we-do/standards/codes-and-manuals/aquatic-code/

## Notes
- Seasonal and species guidance should match your region and climate.
- Government schemes change. Never treat an old document as current eligibility advice: check the issuing authority's official page and application dates.
- For disease, chemicals or medicines, contact a fisheries officer or aquatic animal health professional.
