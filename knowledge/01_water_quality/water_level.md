# Water level and depth

Water level affects more than volume. Shallow ponds heat and cool fast, and less water dilutes waste less. Sudden level changes disturb sediment, change habitat and concentrate pollutants.

## What to watch
- Falling level and leakage.
- Unexpected change after heavy rain, or overflow and embankment damage.
- Depth too low for the stocked species, especially in hot weather.
- Changes at inlets and outlets.

## What the sensor does and does not say
A level sensor measures the water surface relative to where it is mounted. It cannot measure oxygen, water quality, leakage or usable pond volume by itself. Pond dimensions, mounting height, reference level and calibration are needed to interpret it. PondFix stores and shows water level but does not classify it against a limit.

## Response to a falling level
1. Check the reading is steady and not sensor noise or an obstruction.
2. Walk the embankments and outlets for leaks or open drains.
3. Watch temperature and DO, because a lower volume changes both.
4. Check that any replacement water is suitable before adding it.

Low water alone does not diagnose fish disease.
