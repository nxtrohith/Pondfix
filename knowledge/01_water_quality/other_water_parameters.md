# Other water parameters

| Parameter | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Hardness | Reflects calcium and magnesium; relevant to osmoregulation and some species. |
| Carbon dioxide | High levels interfere with breathing, especially when oxygen is low. |
| Hydrogen sulfide | Severe toxic hazard from oxygen-free organic sediment. |
| Transparency | A simple guide to plankton abundance and pond productivity. |
| Turbidity | Many suspended particles can irritate gills and block light. |
| Salinity | Essential for brackish and marine systems and for species with specific salinity needs (for example shrimp). PondFix has no salinity sensor. |
| Nitrate | A later product of the nitrogen cycle; interpretation depends on level, species and system. |

Pond-culture guidance stresses routine monitoring over single readings, and the link between water chemistry, daily oxygen swings, organic matter and fish health.

## Not measured
A parameter the hardware does not measure must be shown as "Not measured". Never assume a pond is safe on a parameter nobody measured. For a prototype, some of these can be entered manually after a water test.
