# When to escalate to an expert

Recommend contacting a fisheries officer, veterinarian or aquatic animal health laboratory immediately when:

- Fish are gasping severely or deaths are widespread.
- Several animals develop severe signs in a short time.
- A serious infectious disease is suspected to be spreading.
- A chemical spill or toxic exposure is suspected.
- Water quality is severely abnormal and the right correction is unclear.
- The farmer is considering an unverified chemical dose or antibiotic.
- A suspected notifiable disease, such as white spot in shrimp, is involved: follow local reporting rules.

## What to tell the farmer to record first
Species and size, how many are affected, when signs began, the speed of spread, recent feeding, weather and water-quality readings, and photos of lesions.

## Biosecurity while waiting
Avoid moving fish, water, nets and gear between ponds. Remove dead fish promptly and safely.
