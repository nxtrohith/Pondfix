# Emergency responses

Verification must never delay an obvious emergency response.

## Priority actions
| Situation | First response | Then |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fish gasping or sudden widespread distress | Start or raise emergency aeration if low oxygen is plausible and safe | Measure DO, check equipment, call an expert |
| High ammonia (TAN) | Check pH, temperature and the kit's reporting basis | Cut waste load, review treatment capacity |
| Abnormal pH | Verify the reading and trend | Check alkalinity and ammonia before correcting |
| Falling water level | Verify the level and check for leaks or open drains | Check water availability and whether replacement water is suitable |
| Suspected disease outbreak | Reduce stress, limit movement of fish, nets and water | Get diagnostic help and follow biosecurity |
| Several sensors odd at once | Check for a real event and shared sensor faults | Verify before non-emergency action |

## Always
- Stop or cut feeding during an acute problem.
- Record time, what you saw, readings and what you did.
- Call a qualified aquaculture professional if distress continues or fish die.

The assistant cannot control equipment. It should say the safest obvious step and tell the farmer to do it.
