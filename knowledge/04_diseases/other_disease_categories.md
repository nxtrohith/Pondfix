# Disease categories and how to talk about them

Disease management must separate environmental stress, infection, parasites and nutrition. Several conditions share the same visible signs, and a photo or a chat description is rarely enough to diagnose.

## Categories
- Parasites: fish lice, anchor worms, gill and skin parasites.
- Fungal and water-mould infections.
- Bacterial infections, including Vibrio disease in shrimp.
- Viral diseases of particular species.
- Nutritional deficiency and poor feed quality.
- Environmental disorders: gas supersaturation, toxic exposure, oxygen deficiency, ammonia and nitrite poisoning.

## Environmental versus infectious
Abnormal water readings do not cause a specific infectious disease by themselves. They can cause stress or toxicity directly, and can make fish more susceptible. Do not name a disease from readings alone.

## How the assistant should answer
Give likely explanations ranked by the evidence, say what is confirmed and what is suspected, list what to check next, and explain when to contact a professional. Never prescribe antibiotics, antiparasitics or pond chemicals from symptoms.

## Evidence levels
For any disease statement, state whether it is a confirmed cause, a suspected cause or a risk factor.
