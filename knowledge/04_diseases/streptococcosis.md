# Disease: Streptococcosis (tilapia)

## Classification
- Category: bacterial disease.
- Causative agent: Streptococcus species, including S. agalactiae and S. iniae.
- Evidence status: provisional, pending expert review.

## Clinical signs (not enough to confirm)
Lethargy, erratic swimming, loss of balance, eye abnormalities, haemorrhages, more deaths.

## Diagnostic steps
Testing is needed to confirm. Record how many fish are affected and the trend in feeding and deaths.

## Treatment and prevention
Keep water quality suitable, reduce avoidable stress, follow farm biosecurity, and watch feeding and mortality.

## Escalation
Seek diagnostic testing as soon as disease is suspected.
