# Disease: Tilapia lake virus disease (TiLV)

## Classification
- Category: viral disease of tilapia.
- Causative agent: tilapia lake virus (TiLV).
- Evidence status: provisional, pending expert review.

## Clinical signs
Lethargy, reduced feeding, abnormal swimming, other whole-body abnormalities and rising deaths. These overlap with environmental stress and other diseases. **AquaGuard must not diagnose TiLV from low activity, temperature or pH.**

## Diagnostic steps
Laboratory testing is required.

## Treatment and prevention
Source fingerlings from reliable suppliers, follow stock movement and quarantine rules, and keep biosecurity. Antibiotics do not treat a virus.

## Escalation
Contact an aquatic animal health professional or lab when TiLV is suspected, and follow local reporting rules.
