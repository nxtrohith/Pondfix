# Disease: Argulosis (fish lice)

## Classification
- Category: external parasite.
- Causative agent: Argulus species (fish lice).
- Evidence status: provisional, pending expert review.

## Clinical signs
Irritation and rubbing against pond surfaces, restlessness, skin damage, haemorrhages or lesions, reduced feeding, abnormal activity. Rohu and catla are reported as susceptible Indian carps.

## Likely causes and risk factors
Infested fingerlings or stock, moving untreated fish between ponds.

## Diagnostic steps
Inspect fish, especially the skin and fins, for visible parasites. Parasite identification needs a qualified person.

## Immediate precautions
Quarantine new stock where practical and avoid moving fish between ponds.

## Treatment and prevention
Inspect fingerlings and fish regularly. Get a confirmed diagnosis before using any antiparasitic treatment.

## Escalation
Contact an aquaculture professional if many fish are affected or lesions become infected.
