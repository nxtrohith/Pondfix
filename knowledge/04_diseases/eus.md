# Disease: Epizootic ulcerative syndrome (EUS)

## Classification
- Category: ulcerative disease of freshwater fish.
- Causative agent: the oomycete Aphanomyces invadans.
- Evidence status: provisional, pending expert review.

## Clinical signs
Reddened or haemorrhagic areas, skin ulcers that can deepen, lethargy, abnormal swimming, secondary infection and deaths in severe outbreaks. A red spot is not enough: injuries, parasites and other infections cause lesions too.

## Water-quality associations
Check water quality and recent environmental changes, but water readings do not confirm EUS.

## Diagnostic steps
Record species, approximate size and when signs began. Photograph lesions and track how cases spread. Confirmation needs a fisheries disease specialist or aquatic animal health lab.

## Immediate precautions
Avoid unnecessary movement of fish, water, nets and equipment between ponds.

## Treatment and prevention
ICAR research notes that treatment is limited. Do not present any single chemical as a guaranteed cure. Follow expert guidance.

## Escalation
Contact a fisheries disease specialist as soon as ulcers appear on more than a few fish.
