# Disease: Lernaeasis (anchor worm)

## Classification
- Category: external parasite.
- Causative agent: Lernaea parasitic copepods, called anchor worms.
- Evidence status: provisional, pending expert review.

## Clinical signs
Visible parasites attached to the body, local inflammation, skin damage and secondary infection, reduced feeding or abnormal activity. ICAR-CIFA work identifies catla as a susceptible host. Do not assume every carp is equally susceptible.

## Diagnostic steps
Inspect fish for attached parasites.

## Immediate precautions
Do not move infested fish between ponds.

## Treatment and prevention
Keep hatchery and pond biosecurity, inspect fish, and use diagnosis-led treatment advised by a professional.

## Escalation
Contact an aquaculture professional if many fish are infested or wounds are infected.
