# Disease: Columnaris

## Classification
- Category: bacterial disease.
- Causative agent: Flavobacterium columnare. Documented in FAO's Nile tilapia profile and in other freshwater fish.
- Evidence status: provisional, pending expert review.

## Clinical signs (non-specific)
Whitish or grey skin patches, fin damage, gill lesions, skin deterioration. Visible lesions alone cannot confirm columnaris.

## Likely causes and risk factors
Stress, crowding, handling injuries, unsuitable water conditions and organic pollution.

## Diagnostic steps
Check water quality, record how many fish and how fast, and get a confirmed diagnosis before treating.

## Immediate precautions
Reduce stress and handling, remove sources of organic pollution, and limit movement of stock and equipment.

## Treatment and prevention
Keep water quality suitable, minimise injury, and keep hygiene good. Do not choose a treatment from appearance alone.

## Escalation
Contact an aquatic animal health professional if lesions spread or deaths rise.
