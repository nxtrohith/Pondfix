# Disease: White spot syndrome in shrimp (WSSV)

## Classification
- Category: serious viral disease of susceptible crustaceans, including cultured shrimp.
- Causative agent: white spot syndrome virus (WSSV).
- Evidence status: provisional, pending expert review.

## Clinical signs
Reduced feeding, lethargy, reddish or pink colour, white spots on the shell in some animals, and fast-rising deaths. White spots are not a diagnosis: other environmental and bacterial conditions can look similar, and infected shrimp may show no spots.

## Diagnostic steps
Testing by a qualified lab is required.

## Immediate precautions
Treat a suspected outbreak as a serious biosecurity problem. Restrict movement of animals, water and contaminated equipment.

## Treatment and prevention
Review stock origin and screening, pond preparation and recent water-quality changes. Do not promise that antibiotics or water-quality fixes cure a viral infection.

## Escalation
Contact an aquatic animal health professional or lab at once, and follow local disease-reporting rules.
