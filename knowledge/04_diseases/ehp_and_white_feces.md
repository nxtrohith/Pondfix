# Disease: Enterocytozoon hepatopenaei (EHP) and white feces syndrome

## Classification
- Category: microsporidian parasite of shrimp, especially Penaeus (Litopenaeus) vannamei and other susceptible penaeids.
- Evidence status: provisional, pending expert review.

## Clinical signs
Slow growth and uneven sizes are the main concern. Some populations also show reduced feeding, soft shells and other changes. EHP can be associated with white feces syndrome, but white feces do not confirm EHP: white feces syndrome has several possible causes.

## Diagnostic steps
Record feed intake, growth rate, size spread and deaths. Check pond-bottom condition, water quality and feeding. Laboratory testing is needed for EHP.

## Treatment and prevention
Review biosecurity, stock sourcing and movement of contaminated material. Use a management plan approved by a veterinarian or specialist.

## Escalation
Get laboratory testing and specialist advice when growth stalls or sizes vary a lot.
