# Disease: Saprolegniosis (water mould)

## Classification
- Category: oomycete (water mould) infection.
- Causative agent: Saprolegnia species.
- Evidence status: provisional, pending expert review.

## Clinical signs
Cotton-like growth on skin or other tissue, and skin damage. It often follows injury or stress.

## Treatment and prevention
Reduce injuries, keep water quality good, and inspect affected fish. Get professional advice before any treatment.

## Escalation
Contact an aquaculture professional if many fish are affected.
