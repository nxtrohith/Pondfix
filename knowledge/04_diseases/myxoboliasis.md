# Disease: Myxoboliasis

## Classification
- Category: parasitic disease.
- Causative agent: Myxobolus and related parasites. It is reported among health problems of Indian carp culture.
- Evidence status: provisional, pending expert review.

## Clinical signs
Vary with the parasite and the tissue affected. They cannot be identified by eye or from water readings.

## Diagnostic steps
Laboratory confirmation is required.

## Treatment and prevention
Use healthy fingerlings, avoid moving contaminated stock and equipment, and follow pond preparation and biosecurity practice.

## Escalation
Seek laboratory testing and professional advice when infection is suspected.
